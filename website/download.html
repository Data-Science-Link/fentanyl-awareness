What the columns mean

Column dictionary for deaths_by_state_month.csv
ColumnWhat it means
YearCalendar year.
MonthCalendar month, written as year-month.
StateUnited States, a state, the District of Columbia, New York City, or Puerto Rico. New York City is separate from New York.
Geographynation, state, city, or territory.
Deaths in the monthDeaths that month when a finished certificate exists. For the United States after the last final year, a preliminary estimate. Blank for a state when CDC has not finished the month. A blank is not a zero.
ConfidenceFinal death certificate, Preliminary estimate, Withheld by CDC, or Not published as a single month.
Preliminary 12-month totalCDC's preliminary total for the 12 months ending that month. This is not the deaths in that month alone, and it should not be added across months.
Preliminary basispredicted or reported, naming which CDC total was used. Recent months use the predicted total.
NoteHow to read the row, including the drug category.
SourceWhich CDC file the numbers came from.

Download deaths_by_month.csv

A number below zero is not a count of deaths. It means a provisional total was revised. The method is written out in how a month is counted.

Column dictionary for deaths_by_month.csv
ColumnWhat it means
MonthThe calendar month, written as year-month.
Estimated deathsDeaths that month. See the next column for whether it is an official count or an estimate.
How this number was producedEither "Official final count" or "Estimated from the change in the 12-month total."
Deaths per 100,000Deaths that month for every 100,000 people. This is not a yearly rate.
Change from the previous monthThis month's deaths minus the month before.
Change from the same month a year earlierThis month's deaths minus that month one year earlier.
Change in the 12-month totalThe arithmetic CDC's rolling total implies. It equals this month minus the same month a year earlier when both totals are complete. Kept so the estimate can be checked.
Official actions that monthActions whose source date falls in the month. Blank means none in the list.
SourceWhere the death number came from.
NoteWhat the category includes, and a warning if an estimate fell below zero.

Fentanyl Awareness reports public counts of synthetic-opioid deaths, including illicit fentanyl. The figures are refreshed every Monday, and a new month is added when CDC publishes one.

These counts are people. Confirm a figure with the agency that published it before using it for a decision.
