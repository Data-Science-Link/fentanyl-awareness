Agency releases that mention fentanyl, each linked to its source, are listed on the announcements page.

Fentanyl Awareness reports public counts of synthetic-opioid deaths, including illicit fentanyl. The figures are refreshed every Monday, and a new month is added when CDC publishes one.

These counts are people. Confirm a figure with the agency that published it before using it for a decision.
