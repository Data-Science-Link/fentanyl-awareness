This site was built because that question was surprisingly hard to answer. The deaths are public, but they are split across CDC files. Recent months are still preliminary. There was no plain page that said whether the toll was rising or falling.

It is also here because the loss is enormous. These counts are people. The page keeps that visible, and then lets you choose what you came for.
