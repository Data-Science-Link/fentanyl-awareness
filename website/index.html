About this project

This portal republishes one official CDC series so anyone can see whether synthetic-opioid deaths are rising or falling, and how states compare on a per-capita basis.

Do not add the monthly rows together. rolling_12_month_deaths is a 12-month ending total, not deaths that occurred in that month. Adding a year’s monthly rows counts the same deaths many times.
What the numbers are
  • Source: CDC Vital Statistics Rapid Release, SODA dataset xkb8-kh2a.
  • Indicator: Synthetic opioids, excl. methadone (ICD-10 T40.4). This includes fentanyl and other synthetics such as tramadol. It is not a fentanyl-only series.
  • Metric: Provisional 12-month ending counts. The month is the end of that window.
  • Time span: 2015 through the latest month CDC has published.
  • Rates: Deaths per 100,000 use ACS 5-year population. If the death year is newer than the latest ACS year, the last available population is carried forward and flagged.
How to read missing values
  • A blank death count means CDC withheld the number (suppression or low data quality). It is not a zero.
  • Use predicted_12_month_deaths when CDC published a delay-adjusted estimate.
  • Louisiana is often absent from T40.4 VSRR when quality thresholds are not met.
  • United States, New York City, and Puerto Rico sit in the same file as states. Filter on geo_type before ranking or summing.
  • New York and New York City are separate CDC reporting areas. Do not add them to a national total that already includes the United States row.

Counts are provisional and are revised as death investigations close. Verify against CDC VSRR before using these figures for policy.
