Are deaths rising or falling?

Loading the monthly counts…

Each point is the estimated number of people who died that month. Where CDC has finished the death certificates, the point is that official count, drawn as a solid line. After the last finished year, the point is estimated from the change in CDC's 12-month total, drawn as a dashed line. Vertical lines are dates of official actions. A line is a date, not proof the action caused a change. The official actions page reads those dates one by one.
