The series does not stop at the last finished year. Where CDC has finished the death certificates, the point is that official count, drawn as a solid line. After that year, the point is a preliminary estimate from the change in CDC's 12-month total, drawn as a dashed line. The estimate stays inside one kind of CDC total, so a switch from the reported total to the predicted total is not counted as deaths in a single month. Vertical lines are public policy actions. A line is a date, not proof the action caused a change. The public policy page reads those dates one by one.

Change over the past year, by state

This map is the percent change in the 12-month total, not the number of deaths in that month. It compares the latest month with the same month a year earlier, using the same kind of CDC total for both. States CDC did not publish are gray. A blank count is left out. It is not treated as zero. Louisiana is gray because CDC does not include it in this series. New York is the state outside New York City. CDC counts the city on its own, and this map does not add the city into the state. The list and the table use the same numbers as the map.
