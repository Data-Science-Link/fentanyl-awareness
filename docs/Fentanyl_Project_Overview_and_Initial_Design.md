# Fentanyl Project Overview and Initial Design

This is the layout brief for the public site. Counts, rates, and dates on the site cite the agency or report that published them. This brief decides which questions the pages answer. It is not a source for a number.

## Introduction / Purpose

* Help Americans with little to no understanding of fentanyl get up to speed on the scope and importance of the recent growth of fentanyl in the US.
* Try to determine if there has been a drop in fentanyl deaths post-policy changes or border enforcement

## Questions/Tabs to Cover:

* What is fentanyl and why is it important?
  * Text description
  * Death toll per 1000 people for various events per year (WWII, WWI, Covid pandemic, vietnam war)
* Is fentanyl use going up or down?
  * Time series of deaths
  * Map of % increase by state
* Who is impacted by, controls, and is responsible for policing fentanyl?
  * Some visual with faces and links to youtube video
  * Histogram of age
  * Bar chart of races impacted vs. race distribution in country
  * Map of cartels
  * Map of supply chain
  * Map of crossings into the US
  * The flow chart graphic of % fentanyl
  * Confiscation % by agency
  * Budget by agency bar chart
* Have recent policies been helpful for reducing the flow or fatalities from fentanyl
* Fentanyl in the news and their sources
